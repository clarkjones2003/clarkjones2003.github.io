Bao HelloPrivacy Policy

Bao Hello does not collect any information about you.

What the game keeps

To remember where you are, the game saves a few things only on your own device:

  • your level, cash and what you've unlocked in the flower shop
  • your settings (sound and vibration on or off)

It is never sent to us or anyone else (it is only included in your own iPhone backups, if you use them). We can't see it, and it's deleted if you delete the app or tap Start over in the game's settings.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or names.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The game works without an internet connection.

Children

Bao Hello is made for everyone, including children. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date below before the new version of the app comes out.
