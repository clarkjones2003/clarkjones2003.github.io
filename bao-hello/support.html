Bao HelloHelp & Support

Run a flower shop as Bao the panda: grab bouquets, serve the line, collect the cash and grow your shop one level at a time.

How to play

  • Walk: drag your thumb anywhere on the screen.
  • Pick up flowers: stand next to a flower stand.
  • Serve: stand in the white box behind the blue counter. Customers take the flower they want.
  • Get paid: walk over the cash they leave.
  • Unlock things: stand on a dashed pad to spend cash on it.
  • Wrong flowers? Toss them in the green trash can by the counter.

Common questions

No sound? Check the silent switch on your iPhone, and the Sound switch in the game's settings (blue gear button).

Want to start again? Open settings, tap Start over, then Yes, start over. This can't be undone.

Lost my progress? Progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.
